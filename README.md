# 📊 U.S. eCommerce Performance Dashboard

An enterprise-grade Business Intelligence and financial analytics repository designed to evaluate **U.S. eCommerce performance**, turnover models, division breakdowns, customer concentration, and complete profit & loss (P&L) metrics down to PBO.

---

## 🚀 Executive Summary & Key Metrics

This repository houses analytical reporting templates, metric breakdowns, and dashboard documentation covering multi-year trends (2017–2019). Key high-level performance indicators include:
* **Net Sales Value (NSV)
* **Net Invoice Price (NIP)
* **Turnover (TO)
* **Gross Margin (GM%)
* **Profit Before Overhead (PBO)
---
## 📑 Dashboard Structure & Report Sections

The accompanying report (`/reports/U.S. eCommerce Dashboard.pdf`) is structured into 11 core analytical tabs

1. **Executive Summary:** High-level overview of total U.S. eCommerce performance
2. **Turnover - by Model:** View Turnover (TO) and USG by business model.
3. **Turnover - Division by Model:** Cross-analysis of TO and USG across divisions and operating models
4. **Turnover - Top 10 Customers by Division:** Identifies top revenue drivers 
5. **Turnover - Division / Category:** Granular performance split across divisions 
6. **Turnover - Mix:** Tree charts exploring business sizing by category per customer and vice versa
7. **Topline:** Deep-dive into NSV, NIP, and TO by customer and category
8. **Brand NSV:** Tracks brand-level NSV performance and Year-over-Year (YoY) growth rates
9. **P&L - By Period:** Monthly and quarterly P&L tracking down to PBO
10. **P&L - By Segment:** Segment-level P&L breakdown by category, customer, or model
11. **GM% Matrix:** Evaluates Gross Margin percentages against eComm category averages

---

## ⚙️ Interactive Features & Navigation Notes

* **Time Period Adjustments:** View data across multiple YoY periods by holding the `Ctrl` key while selecting date filters at the top of each page.
* **Global Filters:** Use the black Filters tab on the right side of the interface to adjust views by division, model, customer, category, or brand Filters apply globally across all tabs
* **Resets:** Click the **'Reset to Default'** button at the top of any page to clear custom filter selection.
* **Brand Data Availability:** Note that brand-level data is tracked specifically at the **NSV level**
